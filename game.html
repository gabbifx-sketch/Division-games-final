About Neon Drift Turbo

Tear through glowing coastal highways at 300 km/h in Neon Drift Turbo. Chain precision power-slides around hairpin turns to charge your nitro boost, unlock 12 synthwave supercars, and climb the global time-trial leaderboard right in your browser.

How to Play

  1. 1 Hold acceleration and steer early before entering glowing corners.
  2. 2 Tap Handbrake to initiate a drift and fill your turbo meter.
  3. 3 Release Drift on straightaways to trigger a supersonic boost.

Controls

Drive & Steer W A S D / Arrows
Drift / Handbrake Spacebar
Touch Controls On-Screen Pedals
